Farm Fresh Mozzarella Cheese

Handcrafted Artisanal Mozzarella Cheese

✓ Available Daily

Product Overview

Our Fresh Mozzarella Cheese is handcrafted daily by skilled cheesemakers right on our farm using morning-milked 100% fresh cow milk. Designed to deliver supreme meltability, elastic stretch, and a rich, creamy flavor profile, Rothes Mozzarella is the favorite choice for pizzeria chefs, restaurants, and gourmet home cooks. We adhere strictly to natural curdling and stretch processes without artificial stabilizers or synthetic emulsifiers.
