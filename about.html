Our Story

From a Small Herd to
Sri Lanka's Finest Dairy

"Dairy farming was always in my blood — founding Rothes Farm in Hatton was the fulfillment of a lifelong dream."

Dairy farming was a cherished family occupation since childhood for our founder, Mr. A. Angamuthu. After establishing a successful career as a wholesale egg trader in Pettah, Colombo, he pursued his lifelong dream in 2003 by purchasing 12 acres of land known as "Rothes 'A'" in Hatton, Sri Lanka, to found Rothes (A) Dairy Farm.

Over two decades of dedication, we have grown into a 240-cow operation producing over 3,000 litres of fresh milk daily. Recognized as a pioneer supplier to Fonterra Brands Lanka (Pvt) Ltd, Rothes (A) Dairy Farm has expanded to produce premium Cow Ghee, Mozzarella Cheese, Artisanal Paneer, Cattle Feed, and Dewatered Cow Dung Organic Manure.

The Founding Dream

Mr. A. Angamuthu acquired 12 acres ("Rothes 'A'") in Hatton, turning a cherished family passion for dairy into Rothes (A) Dairy Farm.

Fonterra Partnership

Became a pioneer fresh milk supplier to Fonterra Brands Lanka (Pvt) Ltd — a landmark quality recognition.

Introduced premium Jersey cattle to our herd, boosting milk fat content and overall quality significantly.
